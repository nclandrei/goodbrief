Questions

Who is VoTriX for?

For two people who need to talk — face to face or at a distance — when at least one is deaf, hard of hearing, mute, doesn’t speak the other’s language or speaks it poorly.

What are the products?

VoTriX — on a single phone, for a face-to-face talk. VoTriX Share — a two-way conversation, each on their own device. VoTriX Call — subtitled phone calls, with an optional web guest. VoTriX Teams — one host and a whole group, each in their own language. VoTriX PC — your own private server, on-premise, for institutions.

What is VoTriX (base)?

The standalone version: live captions, translation and voice right on the phone, no server and no subscription. One-time $200. Works even without internet.

How is Share different from plain VoTriX?

Share is a two-way conversation: you on your device, your guest on theirs (they join with a link or QR code, no install), each in their own language. It comes as an app, a web page, or for a real phone call.

What is VoTriX Teams?

One host speaks once, and many guests follow — each on their phone, in their own language. With raise-a-hand to take the floor. Great for tours, classes, conferences.

What is DoTriX?

An add-on for Share and Teams: during the conversation you send a document (form, contract, record) and the other side receives it translated into their language, with the layout kept intact. $25 = 50 pages, valid 30 days. It’s VoTriX’s unique differentiator.

How do I pay for usage (credits)?

Every Share, Call or Teams license includes 10 hours of talk time to start (valid 30 days). Need more? In Add-ons you pick exactly how many hours you want (from 5 up) at $2 per hour; they add up in your balance and expire 30 days after purchase. No subscription. On-device VoTriX and VoTriX PC use no hours.

Can I try it free?

Yes, no card: VoTriX (on device) has 24h free; Share and Teams have 3 days / 2 hours. (Call and PC have no trial.) Enter your email and get the code instantly.

Does it replace an interpreter?

No. It makes everyday conversation possible, right away. For critical legal or medical situations, a professional interpreter is still recommended.

Is my data safe?

Audio isn’t stored, and plain VoTriX processes everything locally, on the phone. Built with privacy in mind (GDPR).

Does it work without internet?

Plain VoTriX (on-device) works offline too. Share and Teams need a connection, since they link several people.

How many languages?

Dozens of common languages on-device, and 200+ languages on the server-connected products.
