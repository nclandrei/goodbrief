All 16 Traxx Passenger electric locomotives ordered by the Romanian Railway Reform Authority (ARF) have been delivered on schedule

The final locomotive has been handed over to the customer today, and will soon be ready for passenger service, along with the other 15

The contract includes maintenance services for the next 20 years

Traxx Passenger is the first rolling stock in Romania to operate in commercial passenger service under ETCS Level 2

19 November 2026 – Alstom, a global leader in smart and sustainable mobility, announces the official handover of the final Traxx Passenger electric locomotive from the fleet of 16 ordered by the Romanian Railway Reform Authority (ARF). The milestone completes the fleet’s delivery on schedule and marks another important step in the modernisation of passenger rail transport in Romania. The locomotives were ordered under the contract signed with ARF in January 2024, which includes 20 years of associated maintenance services.

"Today’s delivery of the 16th Traxx locomotive – the final unit in the batch, prepared for passenger service – demonstrates that Alstom has fulfilled its commitment to ARF on schedule."

Laurențiu Ivan

Alstom Managing Director for Romania, Bulgaria and the Republic of Moldova

A timely delivery supporting modern passenger rail

The first serial Traxx Passenger locomotive was delivered in April 2026, followed by the progressive introduction of the fleet across Romania. Completing the programme on schedule gives ARF and the national passenger operator access to a modern, high-performance fleet designed to improve the reliability and efficiency of long-distance rail services.

“Today’s delivery of the 16th Traxx locomotive – the final unit in the batch, prepared for passenger service – demonstrates that Alstom has fulfilled its commitment to ARF on schedule. The value of this state-of-the-art rolling stock will be proven over time thanks to its reliability, supported by the maintenance services included in the contract, to be delivered by Alstom personnel within dedicated new maintenance facilities, that are currently being expanded. Furthermore, as the first rolling stock in Romania to operate commercial passenger operations under ETCS Level 2, this fleet marks an important step towards safer, more interoperable, and more efficient rail travel for passengers,” said Laurențiu Ivan, Alstom Managing Director for Romania, Bulgaria and the Republic of Moldova.

Romania’s first passenger operations under ETCS Level 2

The Traxx Passenger locomotive is the first rolling stock in Romania to operate in commercial passenger service under the European Train Control System (ETCS) Level 2. The technology continuously exchanges information between the train and trackside signalling infrastructure, strengthening operational safety and interoperability with the wider European rail network.

The locomotives are equipped with Alstom’s Onvia Cab™ onboard signalling solution. ETCS Level 2 operation is currently active between Buftea and Brazi on the Bucharest–Budapest route operated by CFR Călători, bringing together modern trains and digital infrastructure in commercial passenger service for the first time in Romania.

The four-axle Traxx Passenger electric locomotives are designed for speeds of up to 200 km/h – in Romania being authorized for maximum speed of 160 km/ h – and can haul up to 16 passenger coaches. Their multi-system design supports cross-border operation, while their energy-efficient platform and extended maintenance intervals are designed to improve availability and operational performance.

The contract covers 16 locomotives and 20 years of maintenance and repair services, with the possibility of a further 20-year extension. It builds on Alstom’s long-standing contribution to Romania’s rail sector and its expertise in rolling stock, signalling, electrification and services.
